# Third-party material and terms

This repository contains our own code, protocol files, per-image predictions and analysis outputs. Third-party data and model files are **not redistributed**. `scripts/reproduce.sh` downloads each one from its provider and checks it against the checksum listed here. The only third-party files included are source files whose licences permit unmodified redistribution: Hooker et al. code excerpts and TensorFlow source excerpts (Apache-2.0, with their headers), the airbench94 script (MIT, with its licence) and copies of the dataset licence texts (`provenance/licences/`). Figures that show ImageNet images (`results/figures/fig8_examples.{pdf,png}`, Figure 1 of the article) are not included (`.gitignore`); `results/figures/fig8_examples_selection.json` names the 12 images and `src/fig_examples.py` regenerates the figure from the gated ImageNet download. Licence texts were read first-hand on 2026-09-29, and copies are in `provenance/licences/` with `SHA256SUMS`.
